Common questions

The materials are pink. How do I fix that?

Pink means the materials don't match your project's render pipeline. Each pack's documentation lists the pipelines it supports and how to switch. In Everyday Cars, which uses URP by default, a Built-in project needs RenderPipelines/BuiltIn_Materials.unitypackage from the pack folder: importing it replaces the materials in place, and the prefabs keep working.

Which Unity versions are supported?

Each pack's store page lists the versions it supports. Everyday Cars works with Unity 2022.3 or newer, including Unity 6.

Can I edit the models?

Yes. Models come as FBX files that open in Blender, Maya or any other 3D package. In Everyday Cars the meshes are quad-dominant with solid-colour materials and no UVs; other packs list their topology and texturing on their store pages.

Do the Everyday Cars vehicles drive?

No, the pack is art only, with no driving physics or controller. Wheels and the steering wheel are separate objects with correct pivots, and a small component spins and steers them, so you can connect your own controller.

Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

Yes. Packs are sold under the Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use in your own games and apps but not reselling or sharing the assets on their own. The Asset Store Terms of Service and EULA have the full terms.
