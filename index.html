Everyday Cars – Low Poly Vehicle Pack

Coming soon to the Unity Asset Store

Thirteen road vehicles and a semi-trailer in one glossy, consistent style, from a compact hatchback to a semi truck. Every vehicle has a full interior behind see-through glass, separate wheels, working lamps and three levels of detail, and comes ready as prefabs in 17 colours.

Drop them into traffic, car parks, garages or menus. They share one material library, so a whole street costs few draw calls and a new paint colour is one material.
